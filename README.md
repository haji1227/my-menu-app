## ✨ Why Meal Plan?

Do you store your recipes in Notion, Evernote, or bookmarks, but find it cumbersome to open them just to plan tonight's dinner?
**Meal Plan** acts as a lightweight "cockpit" for your daily cooking.

* **⚡️ Blazing Fast:** No loading times, no login required.
* **🔒 Privacy First:** All data is stored in your browser's **Local Storage**. No external servers, no tracking.
* **🔗 Link Anything:** Paste URLs from Notion, Evernote, YouTube, or recipe sites directly into your menu.
* **📱 PWA Ready:** Install it on your home screen. It feels just like a native app.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Monthly Calendar View:** Drag-free, tap-to-add interface.
* **Smart Suggestions:** Auto-complete menus from your history. The more you use it, the smarter it gets.
* **Meal Types:** Organize by ☀️ Breakfast, 🍱 Lunch, and 🌙 Dinner.
* **Data Backup:** Export your data as a JSON file and save it to Google Drive or iCloud via your device's share sheet.
* **Responsive Design:** Optimized for mobile devices (great on foldables!) and desktops.

## 📖 How to Use

1.  **Open the App:** Access the GitHub Pages link.
2.  **Select a Date:** Tap any date on the calendar.
3.  **Add Menu:** Type the dish name.
    * *Optional:* Paste a URL (Notion, etc.) in the link field.
4.  **Install (Mobile):** Tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu to use it as a full-screen app.

## 💾 Backup & Restore

Since data is stored locally on your device, we recommend backing up regularly.
* **Save:** Tap the `📤 Save` button to export your data. You can save the JSON file to your cloud storage.
* **Load:** Tap `📥 Load` to restore your data from a backup file.
